============================
Purchase Report Payment Term
============================

This module extends ``purchase.report`` and adds payment terms to the purchase analysis report.

Features
========

* Adds ``payment_term_id`` field to ``purchase.report``.
* Includes payment terms in SQL ``SELECT`` and ``GROUP BY``.
* Adds payment terms to search filters and group by options in Purchase Analysis.

Usage
=====

Go to Purchase -> Reporting -> Purchase Analysis, then:

* filter by Payment Terms
* group by Payment Terms
* display Payment Terms in list/pivot views
