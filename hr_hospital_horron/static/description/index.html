HR Hospital

A practical end-to-end hospital workflow module for Odoo. It helps teams move from scattered records to a single process: patient profile, doctor assignment, visit planning, diagnosis control, schedule management, and audit-friendly role access.

Why Teams Use This Module

Faster Front Desk Flow

Create and manage patient visits quickly with date validation, doctor availability checks, and repeatable scheduling workflows.

Clear Clinical Responsibility

Support intern-to-mentor work format with diagnosis approval, patient-doctor history tracking, and controlled access by role.

Operational Visibility

Use list, calendar, pivot, and graph views for visits and diagnoses, plus export/report wizards for managers.

Training-Friendly Architecture

Well-structured models, demo data, tests, and Ukrainian translation make it suitable for learning and team onboarding.

Core Capabilities

Patient & Doctor Records

  • Rich patient profile with contact, insurance, allergies, and blood group
  • Doctor profile with speciality, license metadata, intern/mentor links
  • Doctor reassignment history for traceability

Visit Lifecycle

  • Planned, Done, Cancelled, No Show states
  • Duplicate-per-day prevention for patient-doctor pair
  • Weekend and blocked schedule protection

Diagnosis & Disease Classifier

  • Diagnosis records linked to visits with severity and treatment notes
  • Approval flow for intern-created diagnoses
  • Disease hierarchy with ICD-10 and danger levels

Automation Wizards

  • Visit rescheduling
  • Mass doctor reassignment
  • Disease report generation
  • Patient card export (JSON/CSV)
  • Bulk doctor schedule generation

Typical Workflow

  1. Register doctor specialities and doctors (including intern-mentor relationships).
  2. Create patient cards and assign personal doctors.
  3. Plan visits and validate date/time availability automatically.
  4. Record diagnoses and complete mentor approval when required.
  5. Use reports and exports for management and operational control.

Quick Start

  1. Install the module from the Apps menu.
  2. Open Settings -> Users & Companies -> Users and assign Hospital roles.
  3. Create specialities, doctors, and patients.
  4. Schedule visits and start recording diagnoses.

Demo data is included to let you test workflows right after installation.
